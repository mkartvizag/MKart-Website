Install MKart Admin App

Install MKart Admin on your phone's home screen for quick 1-tap access with full camera and offline support!

Android (Google Chrome)
  1. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) at top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap Install to place the MKart Admin icon on your phone!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Tap the Share button () at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Tap Add at top right. MKart Admin is now on your iPhone!
